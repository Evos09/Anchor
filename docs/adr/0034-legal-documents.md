# ADR-0034: Terms of Service and Privacy Policy need a lawyer

- Status: Accepted (Update #1, 2026-10-08)
- Date: 2026-10-08

## Decision

`docs/legal/terms-DRAFT.md` and `docs/legal/privacy-DRAFT.md` are
**drafts written without legal review**, marked as such on every page.
They describe what the software does (local by default, BYOK direct to
the provider, the proxy's metadata-only record, the caps, no telemetry,
no training on user content). Neither may be published, linked from the
app, or accepted by users until a qualified lawyer has drafted or
reviewed them for the jurisdictions Anchor is offered in (including
GDPR/UK GDPR and the providers' flow-down terms, e.g. acceptable-use
policies). The free tier stays off until then, independently of
ADR-0032's gate.
